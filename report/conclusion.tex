Nuclear fission can be quite hard to simulate, as there are many possible fission reactions and many outside factors that determine the efficiency of the fission reactions. Thus we had to simulate a rather simplified system. Despite the simplification, we did get some very good results. All code works as intended and the results of the tasks give interesting insights.

If we look at the way the critical mass behaves for different scale factors, we can see that the results could be expected to blow up for scale factors smaller than 1, because you divide by the scale factor squared to get the depth \eqref{depth}, making this a factor that grows incredibly fast for small numbers. But despite the depth becoming very big, the width becomes quite small, making this a long but thin beam and thus giving the neutrons more opportunities to escape the mass.
